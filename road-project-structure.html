ROAD ARCHITECTURE RULES

These rules keep the structure consistent across all future road projects.

Stationing is primary.Left/right is defined relative to increasing station direction.
Shouldering is optional.No shoulder records are created unless the project actually includes them.
Components are independent.Each lane or shoulder can have a different station range and planned quantity.
Map is intentionally simple.Project location and saved geometry are shown without drawing or editing tools.
