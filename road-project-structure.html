ROAD ARCHITECTURE RULES

These rules keep the structure consistent across all future road projects.

Stationing is primary.Left/right is defined relative to increasing station direction.
Shouldering is optional.No shoulder records are created unless the project actually includes them.
Components follow stationing.Each lane or shoulder can have its own station range, but its map geometry is derived from the primary alignment.
Alignment is the source of truth.Road sections and work components are generated from station ranges on the primary alignment; they are not independent drawn lines.
